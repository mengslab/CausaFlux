# Biological-validation release status

CausaFlux v1.4.0 executes three preregistered hypotheses on unchanged public SEA-AD metadata. ACT donors form the discovery cohort and non-overlapping ADRC Clinical Core donors form the replication cohort.

## Established

- Three preregistered primary associations supported in discovery and independent source-cohort replication.
- Cross-definition sensitivity analysis across Gabitto 2024, Travaglini 2026, and Kana 2026 CPS estimates.
- Comparison with established nonparametric, parametric, correlation, and adjusted-regression methods.
- Manuscript-quality figures, panel-level source data, methods, manifests, and claims ledger.

## Not established

- AMP-AD external-dataset replication.
- HTAN spatial cancer biological validation.
- DepMap–PRISM–LINCS perturbational validation.
- Molecular neural–glial validation.
- Causal, therapeutic, diagnostic, prognostic, or clinical-guidance claims.
