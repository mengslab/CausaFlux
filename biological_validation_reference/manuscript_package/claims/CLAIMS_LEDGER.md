## BV-SEA-AD-001 — replicated_association
The preregistered association was supported in the discovery cohort and replicated in an independent SEA-AD source cohort. APOE epsilon-4 carriers have higher continuous pseudo-progression scores than non-carriers. This is an observational association, not evidence of causality or clinical utility. External-dataset replication remains pending.
