BV-SEA-AD-001 replicated_association The preregistered association was supported in the discovery cohort and replicated in an independent SEA-AD source cohort. APOE epsilon-4 carriers have higher continuous pseudo-progression scores than non-carriers. This is an observational association, not evidence of causality or clinical utility. External-dataset replication remains pending.
BV-SEA-AD-002 replicated_association The preregistered association was supported in the discovery cohort and replicated in an independent SEA-AD source cohort. Donors with dementia have higher continuous pseudo-progression scores than donors without dementia. This is an observational association, not evidence of causality or clinical utility. External-dataset replication remains pending.
BV-SEA-AD-003 replicated_association The preregistered association was supported in the discovery cohort and replicated in an independent SEA-AD source cohort. Increasing AD neuropathological-change stage is monotonically associated with higher continuous pseudo-progression score. This is an observational association, not evidence of causality or clinical utility. External-dataset replication remains pending.
