# Research Governance

CausaFlux is intended for mechanistic research and experimental prioritization.

## Required practices for real studies

- prospectively define outcomes and decision times;
- use biological replicates and donor-level validation;
- document causal assumptions and negative controls;
- distinguish exploratory, replicated, perturbational, and clinically validated evidence;
- assess unmeasured confounding and out-of-distribution extrapolation;
- validate assays and biomarkers in independent cohorts;
- review therapeutic and experiment recommendations with domain experts;
- protect human data under institutional and legal requirements.

## Prohibited interpretation of packaged demos

Synthetic rankings are not evidence of target validity, drug efficacy, biomarker performance, APOE effects, immune mechanisms, or neural degeneration mechanisms.
